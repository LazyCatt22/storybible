**Purpose.** A working memory between sessions. Everything here is a decision we have agreed on but held out of the bible either because it isn't ready to be surfaced, or because it depends on downstream decisions still open, or because we want to leave it flexible until the writing tests it.

**Rules for this doc.**

## 1. FTL — One Substrate, Four Systems

**Date locked:** 2026-07-30
**Status:** Provisional between us. Not in bible.

**Why we locked it.** We needed a working model for how the four transit systems relate to each other before doing FTL math. We agreed that "four unrelated technologies" is flatter than "one deep truth expressed four ways." This gives us one underlying assumption to work against, without committing to a mechanism.

**What we're keeping (clean, unchanged):**

- One deep substrate exists in the universe.
- Four FTL systems touch it in different ways. How each system interacts with the substrate is an open question, not a locked answer.
- Nothing about who else touches the substrate — Zenith, Choir, Navigators, Death & Fear, Devansh's power — is inferred from the substrate model. Those are their own separate questions.

**The four systems as they stand:**

1. **The bridge** — some kind of persistent structure that uses the substrate to connect two galaxies.
2. **The Soren-en gates** — some kind of local structure that uses the substrate to connect two points inside Andromeda.
3. **Nyralis observation-collapse FTL** — some kind of consciousness-driven mechanism.
4. **Velakhar engine-FTL** — Alcubierre-shape warp, operating in ordinary spacetime; may or may not touch the substrate at all.

## 2. Velakhar Population — Two Numbers Plus a Distribution Rule

**Date locked:** 2026-07-30
**Status:** Provisional between us. Not in bible. Current bible still says 40–60 million at main story, which is wrong and needs to be replaced with the numbers below when we do the pass.

**Why we locked it.** The current bible number (40–60 million across 3–5 systems) collapses under any real anthropological math. A civilization abandoned from billions of Imperial citizens at BFF 1,300, grinding through 1,300 years of alien war down to a survivor population, then growing for 1,200 years post-Halven with a real doctrine and reconquests, ends up at billions — not tens of millions. 40–60M would make the 8%-under-arms military 3–5M, which cannot crew a real interstellar fleet, cannot hold frontier worlds, and cannot support 90,000 Lan-kary as an elite (they would be 0.15–0.22% of the population, which is a state religion, not an elite tier). The reconquest logic also fails at 40–60M — there is no population to move into reconquered worlds.

**Two numbers plus a distribution rule:**

1. **AFF 0 population:** ~75 million (range 50–100M). Battered survivor civilization, small handful of worlds. Starting point for post-Halven growth curve.
2. **Main story population:** ~2 billion (range 1.5–2.5B). Mature power, mostly concentrated in Soren-en system.
3. **Distribution rule:** Soren-en system holds ~60%, Maren 3 system ~12%, other controlled systems and frontier ~28% combined. Halven is pilgrimage-scale, not settlement-scale.

**Downstream consequences (for reference, not locked):**
- 8% under arms at 2B = ~160M military — real interstellar-fleet scale.
- 90,000 Lan-kary = 0.056% of military — tiny fraction by headcount, decisive by capability. Correct ratio for a demigod-tier elite.
- Growth curve is not linear: slow first ~200 AFF, accelerating through Lan-kar reconquest era (~AFF 200–800), plateau in the last 200–300 years as Choir go near-extinct.

## 3. Velakhar — Cultural Loosening in the Last 200 Years

**Date locked:** 2026-07-30
**Status:** Provisional between us. Not in bible. This is Book Two political spine material — writer's-eye framing that shapes scenes but should not be dumped into bible entries wholesale.

**Why we locked it.** The earlier framing that "religious discipline holds the culture no matter what" was too clean and closed off Book Two conflict by accident. A real 1,200-year-old civilization does not stay static. The last 200 years — Choir specifically ending, gate network mature (400 years old at main story), Theven Maad in Marthar leadership — is exactly when a rigid civilization softens at its edges even while its center stays hard. This entry captures the layered picture so the writing has real tension to work with, not a monolith.

**Framing paragraph.** A real 1,200-year-old civilization does not stay static, and the last 200 years — with the Choir specifically ending, gate network mature, and Theven Maad in Marthar leadership — is EXACTLY when a rigid civilization softens on its edges even while its center stays hard.

### What actually loosens in the last 200 years — the layered picture

**Civilian life loosens materially.**

- Peace dividend on the Choir front means fewer Velakhar personally know someone who died to a Choir voice-attack. Third-generation post-Choir civilians experience the emotional dampening as *inherited discipline*, not *lived defense*. Which is different.
- Population growth means the civilian-to-military ratio shifts. If ~8% under arms was the baseline, that number may be dropping slightly — not because military scale is shrinking (it's growing), but because civilian population is growing faster. More farmers, more engineers, more artists, more traders, more clerks per Marthar soldier.
- Art expands. The bible said *"artists usually quiet, often about the dead."* That was true for centuries. In the last 200 years, art gets less quiet. Some artists work in landscape, in architecture, in liturgical embellishment. The Vaeltharn treats this warily.
- Music tests the boundary. Valkry forbids sustained sung vowels, and the Sacred Register reserves the one exception for the priesthood. Young musicians push against this — instrumental music expands, sung liturgy for laypeople remains forbidden, but the *space between spoken and sung* is where interesting things happen.
- Marriage loosens at the edges. Chosen men + command-track women remains the elite pattern, but ordinary Velakhar increasingly marry across worlds, across specialties, sometimes for love as well as institutional continuity. The old pattern holds for the top; the middle becomes more fluid.

**Faith itself gets more personal, less institutional.**

- The Vaeltharn Temple's grip on daily civic life weakens as the Leadership pillar's administrative reach grows. This is what Saa quietly engineered when she instigated the Leadership pillar to counterweight the Temple. Theven Maad is the harvest of that seed.
- Some Velakhar experience the Quiet Lord as a personal presence rather than a Temple-mediated doctrine. This is nascent, and the Vaeltharn frowns on it, but it exists in the last generation or two. Precedent: the Protestant Reformation, or the Bhakti movement in medieval India — direct devotion vs. mediated priesthood, always in tension.
- Younger scholars in Vaeltharn schools ask questions their elders would not have asked. Some of them read old Imperial-era religious material (from before the Velakhar existed) that has surfaced through trade smuggling. This is theologically dangerous and mostly kept quiet.

**Xenophobia loosens at the edges too — but unevenly.**

- The "long question" still runs, but different worlds now interpret it differently. A frontier world with a labor shortage may be more permissive with refugee acceptance than the old Soren-en standard. Vaeltharn priests on Soren-en don't like this.
- Trade smugglers are now a permanent fixture — the bible confirms Imperial rediscovery of the Velakhar happened through them. Which means there are also Velakhar smugglers going the other direction. Those Velakhar are not being executed — they are being *tolerated*, which is a huge cultural shift from where the order was 400 years ago.
- Refugees from Imperial-side collapses (there are constant regional catastrophes in the Empire) sometimes end up in Velakhar space. Acceptance rates are rising slowly.

### What tightens instead — the load-bearing counterweight

The military and technological fronts *harden* in the same period.

- **Kharvurn** expands. New shipbuilding programs, larger fleet, more sophisticated tactical doctrine. The Drennvar's classified observation-collapse FTL research is grinding forward, closing the gap with Imperial FTL. Lan-kary ships already outrun standard Kharvurn hulls (~1200–1500c vs. ~1000c).
- **Korven** institutional complexity peaks. More workshops, more universities graduating engineers, more classified programs. Saa's hand is heaviest here.
- **Lan-kary** grow in institutional weight. The Nine are contemporaries in Saa and Devansh's subjective time but multi-century figures in Velakhar time. Their authority as demigod-tier grows generation on generation.
- **Faith at the elite tier stays rigid.** The Vaelkar and the Halvethun and the Marthar leadership remain locked in doctrine. The elite tier holds while the middle of the culture loosens.

### The tension this produces — Book Two political spine

- **Vaeltharn vs. Leadership**: this tension the bible already flags as the defining internal Velakhar politics. Theven Maad is the current pro-Leadership tipping point. Loosening civilian life is *good* for the Leadership pillar and *bad* for the Vaeltharn's institutional dominance.
- **Elite vs. mass**: the Lan-kar and the Vaelkar stay rigid; the ordinary Velakhar are quietly liberalizing. A generation gap that has real political weight.
- **Homeworld vs. frontier**: Soren-en holds the strict doctrine; frontier worlds accept refugees, run their long-question interpretations more loosely, tolerate smugglers who bring goods.
- **Devansh's absence complicates everything**: the Quiet Lord doctrine says he will return to lead the war that ends the Empire. Every year he does not return, the Vaeltharn's institutional authority takes a small hit and the Leadership's civic authority gains slightly.

This is the real Velakhar in the main story. Not a monolith. **A civilization that has succeeded enough to start softening at its edges, and whose elite is fighting a quiet cultural war against that softening.**
